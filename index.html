What we handle

Most people only need a lawyer a handful of times in their life. Whichever reason brought you here, we have likely seen it before, and we will tell you plainly what your options are.

Family Law

Divorce, child custody, domestic violence, court marriage, and dowry cases, handled with a focus on keeping things civil and moving your life forward. We take the time to understand what matters most to you and your family before deciding on an approach, whether that means negotiating a fair settlement or preparing thoroughly for court.

How we work

Direct communication

You will always know who is handling your case and how to reach them directly. There are no answering services standing between you and your advocate, and no runaround when you need an update. If something changes in your matter, you hear it from us first.

Clear pricing

We explain costs up front, in plain language, before any work begins. You will know what a matter is likely to cost and why, with no surprise invoices along the way. If circumstances change and costs need to be revisited, we discuss it with you before proceeding.

Local knowledge

Years of practice in the Lucknow courts means we know the process, the procedure, and how things actually get done here. That familiarity with local judges, court staff, and filing systems often makes the difference between a matter that drags on and one that moves efficiently.
